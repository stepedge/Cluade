# StepEdge / IT Net: Online Sales Presence and Buyer-Facing Weaknesses (South Africa, Oct 2026)

Method note: Neither the target sites nor third-party sites could be fetched directly. WebFetch returned DNS "ENOTFOUND" for stepedge.co.za, and also for dnb.com and dns.google, so this is a sandbox restriction and NOT evidence that the site is down. Everything below comes from web search results and search-engine snippets. Snippets can be stale or taken out of context. "Verified" here means seen in an indexed snippet. It does not mean the live page was checked.

## 1. What is "IT Net" and how does it relate to StepEdge?

### Takeaway
IT Net is **itnet.co.za**, a second online IT store for home and business buyers. Its footer says it is "operated by Stepedge (Pty) Ltd", and it uses the same phone number and Edenvale location as StepEdge. It is effectively a duplicate storefront under a different brand name, and its search visibility is very weak.

### Cited Findings
- itnet.co.za calls itself an online IT store for home and business in South Africa, and its footer says it is operated by Stepedge (Pty) Ltd (search snippet) — [IT Net contact page](https://itnet.co.za/contact-us/)
- The IT Net contact page title reads: "Need IT help? Contact IT Net today. Call, email, WhatsApp, or use our online form for support, repairs, quotes, and sales. Serving South Africa." It offers support, repairs, sales and managed services, and lets customers book a technician online — [IT Net contact](https://itnet.co.za/contact-us/)
- IT Net shop categories in the snippets: notebooks, peripherals and accessories, CPO, CCTV and security, smart home, and graphics cards. It promises manufacturer warranty, secure checkout and nationwide delivery. It lists Edenvale, Gauteng and +27 11 023 8740, the same number StepEdge uses — [IT Net](https://itnet.co.za/contact-us/); [StepEdge contact](https://stepedge.co.za/contact/)
- StepEdge's own homepage snippet uses the same promise wording: "manufacturer warranty on all products, secure online checkout and nationwide delivery" — [StepEdge home](https://stepedge.co.za/)
- A search for "itnet.co.za product price R" returned no itnet.co.za pages. The results were taken by unrelated ITnet brands: ITnet S.r.l. in Italy (IT.net, data centres), ITNET in Kochi, India (itnetinfo.com), ITNET Technologies in France and Finland, and Incredible Technologies' ITNet gaming platform — [Crunchbase ITnet](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/itnet-5e3b); [itnetinfo.com](https://itnetinfo.com/#products); [itnet-technologies.com](https://www.itnet-technologies.com/en); [datacentermap ITnet](https://www.datacentermap.com/c/itnet/)
- A query for "IT Net" with Edenvale or Gauteng IT solutions returned no IT Net page. It returned rival Edenvale IT firms instead: IN2IT, Ativo ICT, Blucom, and MyIT Solutions — [Bizcommunity IN2IT](https://www.bizcommunity.com/Company/IN2IT); [Bizcommunity Ativo ICT](https://www.bizcommunity.com/Company/AtivoICT); [Mustek locator MyIT](https://mustek.co.za/online-locator/109773/myit-solutions/)

### Inferences
- **(Inferred)** "IT Net" is a generic name that several larger international brands already hold, so it is very hard to rank for. Someone searching "IT Net" is unlikely to land on itnet.co.za. Only the contact page showed up, and only when the query combined the brand with StepEdge or Edenvale terms.
- **(Inferred)** Two stores run by one company, with matching phone, location and warranty wording, split the owner's limited SEO authority, reviews and content effort. Google may also treat the matching boilerplate and product feeds as duplicate content, which would weaken both sites. A buyer who sees both brands with one phone number may question which business is the real one.

### Gaps
- No itnet.co.za product pages, prices, launch date, or product count were indexed or visible, so whether IT Net carries the same SKUs and prices as StepEdge could not be checked.
- It is unknown whether IT Net has a Google Business Profile or any social profiles.

The positioning stretches across hardware retail, managed IT, software and web, and B-BBEE. Snippets point to template leftovers on the shop page and to mixed legal entities and addresses.

### Inferences
- **(Inferred)** If the placeholder "Shop category here with product list" is visible on the live page, it looks unfinished and damages trust on the main shop page.
- **(Inferred)** The same "5 in stock" on unrelated items suggests a default stock value rather than real inventory. Buyers comparing stores may doubt it, or order and then face delays.
- **(Inferred)** The service lines (web design, hosting, remote support, POS) don't appear to have their own indexed landing pages with prices or packages. That fits the earlier finding that the site does not rank for "website design Edenvale"-type queries. No page targets those terms.
- **(Inferred)** The "Knowledge" section appears to have no content, so it adds no SEO value.

### Gaps
- The live page's design, checkout flow, payment options (PayFast, EFT, card), SSL, speed and mobile experience could not be checked.
- The full product count, and whether prices include VAT, are unknown.

## 5. Brand and message consistency issues, plus red flags

### Takeaway
The online identity is fragmented. There are two storefront brands (StepEdge and IT Net), at least four legal-entity names, three or four addresses, and three or four phone numbers. Category descriptions conflict: IT hardware retailer, MSP, software and web agency, telecoms, B-BBEE partner, and on ProCompare a marketing and SEO agency. Buyers and Google both get mixed signals about who and where the business is.

### Cited Findings
- **Entity names:**
  - Terms page: "Stepedge Telecommunications (Pty) Ltd, Registration number 2011/000797/07", plus STEPEDGE AFRICA (Pty) Ltd trading as StepEdge — [Terms](https://stepedge.co.za/terms-and-conditions/)
  - D&B and ZoomInfo: "Stepedge (Pty) Ltd" — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/stepedge-pty-ltd/453233699)
  - IT Net footer: "Stepedge (Pty) Ltd" — [IT Net](https://itnet.co.za/contact-us/)

  - 157 Van Riebeeck Avenue, Edenvale (terms page, legal service address) — [Terms](https://stepedge.co.za/terms-and-conditions/)
  - 3 Willow Rd, Edenvale 1618 (ZoomInfo and D&B) — [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com/c/stepedge-pty-ltd/453233699)
  - A further, different address on ProCompare — [ProCompare](https://www.procompare.co.za/providers/stepedge-pty-ltd)
- **Phones:**
  - +27 11 023 8740 (site and IT Net) — [Contact](https://stepedge.co.za/contact/)

- A strength the site underuses: the B-BBEE Level 1 page ranks, and the About page claims 100% Black and woman-owned. That matters for corporate and public-sector procurement — [B-BBEE page](https://stepedge.co.za/b-bbee-level-1-it-partner/); [About](https://stepedge.co.za/about/)

### Inferences
- **(Inferred)** Mismatched name, address and phone details across directories are a known drag on Google local ranking, and they likely help explain why StepEdge doesn't show up for "IT support or website design Edenvale" while rivals with consistent Bizcommunity and Mustek listings do.
- **(Inferred)** The strongest evidence-backed positioning is "B-BBEE Level 1, 100% Black woman-owned IT supplier for corporate and public-sector procurement in Gauteng". That is where StepEdge already ranks and where price premiums are tolerated. Retail hardware sold to price-comparing buyers and IT Net's "home" retail angle both compete where StepEdge is weakest: price, reviews and brand recognition.
- **(Inferred)** Running StepEdge and IT Net side by side doubles the work while both sites stay thin. Consolidating the effort, or setting a clear split such as StepEdge for B2B and procurement and IT Net for consumers, would reduce confusion.
- **(Inferred)** Likely reasons for the drop to one or two inbound calls, ranked by strength of evidence:
  1. Prices are not competitive on comparable SKUs (strong evidence).
  2. Reviews and social proof are near-absent (strong evidence that none are visible).
  3. No local landing pages exist for services (moderate evidence).
  4. Name, address and phone details conflict, which weakens local SEO (strong evidence of the inconsistency, inferred effect).
  5. The shop looks unfinished: placeholder text and uniform stock figures (moderate evidence, snippet-based).
  6. Brand effort is split across two sites (moderate evidence).

### Gaps
- Whether the registration number 2011/000797/07 matches "Stepedge Telecommunications" on CIPC could not be checked.
- The live site's SSL, speed, mobile UX and checkout could not be audited (fetch blocked).
- Traffic, Search Console or analytics data would be needed to separate "not found" from "found but not converting". Only the owner has access to that data.
