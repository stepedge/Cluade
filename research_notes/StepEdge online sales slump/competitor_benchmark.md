# Competitive Benchmark: Gauteng IT Resellers, MSPs and Web/Hosting Agencies vs StepEdge (Oct 2026)

> Method note: Every attempt to fetch competitor pages directly (WebFetch and curl) failed with DNS/proxy errors in this environment. All findings below come from web-search result snippets and summaries of competitor pages, directories (ProCompare, TechBehemoths, infoisinfo) and tender portals. Nearly all of it is the companies' own marketing claims, not independently verified. Google Maps review counts, Facebook, LinkedIn and TikTok presence, and live Google Ads results could NOT be observed. See the Gaps sections.

## Q1. Competitor-by-competitor profile: headline offer, pricing, lead capture, trust signals, channels

### Takeaway
The competitors who rank fall into three clear groups. (1) B-BBEE Level 1 "procurement-first" hardware resellers (AXLTEC, Black Barter, GTL Telecoms) lead with CSD numbers, Level 1 status and OEM brand names and talk to government and corporate buyers. (2) Local web agencies (Webbon, SAWAPRO, Pixel Flux and others) win on published low entry prices (R3,000 sites, R99/month hosting) and on a dedicated landing page for each suburb, e.g. "Website Design Edenvale". (3) MSPs (ASG, PursuIT/PSIT) sell monthly per-seat retainers with a free assessment as the hook. StepEdge offers products in all three groups, but none of these competitors tries to be all three at once.

## Q2. Which packaging works: fixed bundles, retainers, website+hosting, finance/rental

### Takeaway
The market has settled on three package types: (a) low entry website prices that already include domain, hosting and email (R3,000 to R8,500); (b) hosting priced per month (R99 to R200/month); (c) managed IT billed per user per month (roughly R450 to R1,500/user excl. VAT, on 12-month terms). For hardware, rent-to-own and subscriptions are common in South Africa, but specialist rental firms dominate them rather than IT resellers.

### Cited Findings
- Website + hosting bundles: Webbon's R3,000 site includes domain, hosting and email setup — [Webbon](https://www.webbon.co.za/website-design-edenvale/); hosting from R99/month — [SAWAPRO](https://www.sawapro.co.za/website-designers-in-edenvale/); R200/month web and email hosting — [Webbon](https://www.webbon.co.za/website-design/); R1,200/year — [AUTS](https://www.artistsunderthesun.co.za/)

### Inferences
- Website pricing in Edenvale has become a race to R3,000, so StepEdge is unlikely to win on build price alone. A "Website + hosting + M365 email + remote support" monthly bundle, or a "website on subscription" plan, would stand out because none of the local agencies found sells IT support as well.
- In managed IT, a published per-user price (e.g. "from R450–R550/user") plus a free assessment matches what the stronger local MSPs do. StepEdge can attach hardware, CCTV and POS to that, which pure MSPs cannot.
- None of the resellers found (AXLTEC, Black Barter) advertised finance or rental. StepEdge could offer laptops "from R/month" via a rental partner (e.g. Rentworks or Qrent) or bundle devices into a per-seat "device-as-a-service" price.

### Gaps
- No evidence found on which package converts best (no conversion data is published). Bundled CCTV/POS pricing from Gauteng competitors was not found. No Edenvale agency was confirmed to offer a "website on monthly subscription with no upfront cost".

## Q3. How B-BBEE Level 1 suppliers market to procurement buyers

### Takeaway
They put compliance credentials front and centre: Level 1 and "100% Black-owned / youth-owned" in the page title or hero, the CSD supplier number in plain text, OEM brand names with an "OEM authorised" claim, a procurement email address, and named target buyers (government departments, municipalities, corporates). Tender documents require CSD registration and tax compliance as minimum gates.

### Cited Findings
- AXLTEC puts "B-BBEE Level 1" in its site title, claims "CSD registered, OEM authorised", names government, municipalities and corporates as customers, and gives a procurement@ email — [AXLTEC](https://axltec.co.za/)
- Black Barter publishes its CSD number MAAA1678685, "CSD Verified", Level 1, and "100% Black-owned, youth-led" — [Black Barter](https://blackbarter.co/)
- GTL Telecoms leads with "100% Black Youth Owned" — [GTL About](https://gtltelecoms.co.za/about)
- Datacentrix's PR stresses its 8th consecutive year at Level 1, 100% Black ownership, designated-supplier status and ISO certifications — [ITWeb](https://www.itweb.co.za/article/datacentrix-marks-100-black-ownership-retains-level-one-b-bbee-status-and-multiple-iso-certifications/raYAyMorRRo7J38N); Evernex publicises its yearly Level 1 renewal — [Evernex](https://evernex.com/news/evernex-awarded-level-1-b-bbee-certification-for-another-year/)
- Tender requirements: CSD registration is compulsory in municipal IT tenders (e.g. a 2023 IT-equipment bid) — [eTenders](https://www.etenders.gov.za/home/Download/?blobName=18297778-f077-4209-a239-0b8f24e49a5c.pdf&downloadedFileName=IT+Equipment+Final.pdf); Kouga Municipality's 2026 laptop/desktop tender requires "CSD registered, tax compliant, attended compulsory briefing" — [Tenders-SA](https://www.tenders-sa.org/sa-tenders/tender/206-2026-supply-and-delivery-of-laptops-and-desktops-for-a-period-of-two-2-years-cmtks4z3735k7ldlmziln9kb5); City of Johannesburg ICT03/2024/2025 covers ICT support, hardware, software and security — [CoJ tender](https://mbus.joburg.org.za/ref/procurement/tenders/ICT03-2024-2025%20ICT%20Support%20Services,%20Consulting%20Services%20Hardware,%20Software%20and%20Security%20Tender%20Document.pdf); aggregators listed 72 open computer/server/networking tenders as of 1 Oct 2026 — [TenderBulletins](https://tenderbulletins.co.za/tender-category/supplies-computer-equipment/)

### Inferences
- StepEdge already has a Level 1 page, but to match AXLTEC and Black Barter it should show its CSD MAAA number, a downloadable B-BBEE certificate or affidavit, OEM letters for named brands, and a "Procurement / RFQ" page with a procurement@ address and a fast RFQ turnaround promise. Being in Edenvale, near the many corporate and industrial buyers in Ekurhuleni, is a geographic differentiator the Soweto- and Pretoria-based Level 1 rivals cannot claim.
- Actively bidding on tenders (via eTenders and aggregators) is a separate channel from online retail sales and may be the faster route to closed sales.

### Gaps
- No evidence found on whether competitors list Supplier Development / ESD offerings, or which OEM partner tiers they actually hold.

## Q4. Gaps and opportunities competitors leave open

### Takeaway
No competitor found combines all of these: Edenvale location, Level 1 procurement credentials, online hardware sales, websites/hosting, managed IT, CCTV and POS. Each competitor covers one or two. The common weaknesses are thin third-party reviews, unpublished MSP pricing, no hardware finance offers, and dated SEO-template copy.

### Cited Findings
- ASG's public review footprint is minimal: one TechBehemoths review, and a "98%" satisfaction figure from its own survey — [TechBehemoths](https://techbehemoths.com/company/asg-it-support-services)
- PSIT does not publish per-seat prices — [PSIT](https://www.psit.co.za/it-support-edenvale/)
- SAWAPRO's Edenvale pages are dated and keyword-stuffed; NETCONFIG's Edenvale page is about 998 days old — [SAWAPRO](https://www.sawapro.co.za/website-designers-in-edenvale/); [Netconfig](https://www.netconfig.co.za/it-companies-edenvale/)
- The Level 1 resellers found are based in Soweto (AXLTEC) and Pretoria (GTL) — [AXLTEC](https://axltec.co.za/); [GTL Contact](https://gtltelecoms.co.za/contact)
- ProCompare aggregates Edenvale leads for web design and CCTV, with ratings shown (e.g. TTK 4.6) — [ProCompare](https://www.procompare.co.za/web-design/edenvale)

### Inferences (opportunities for StepEdge)
1. "One local partner" bundles, e.g. "Small Office Starter: website + hosting + M365 email + remote support for R X/month" or "Retail Starter: POS + CCTV + IT support". None of the agencies or MSPs found offer these combinations.
2. Publish anchor prices on every service page: website from R X, hosting from R X/month, IT support from R X/user/month, CCTV kits from R X.
3. Build suburb and service landing pages (Edenvale, Bedfordview, Kempton Park, Germiston, Boksburg) for IT support, CCTV, website design and POS.
4. Create a free-audit lead magnet (an IT and security health check) with a named value, plus a 30-day risk-reversal guarantee, copying the ASG pattern.
5. Add a procurement hub: CSD number, B-BBEE certificate, OEM letters, RFQ form, and "Level 1 supplier in Ekurhuleni" positioning, then actively bid on tenders.
6. Offer hardware as a monthly payment through a rental partner.
7. Run a review drive on Google Business Profile and get listed on ProCompare: competitors' review counts look thin, so a modest number of reviews could stand out.

### Gaps
- Could not verify paid-search competition (which competitors bid on "IT equipment supplier", "CCTV installation Edenvale" and so on) or social channel activity. A manual check of the SERPs and Ads Transparency Center is recommended.
