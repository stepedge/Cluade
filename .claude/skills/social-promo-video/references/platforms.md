# Platform notes: LinkedIn and TikTok (plus WhatsApp, Reels, Shorts)

Platform limits change. Treat the numbers below as safe defaults, not hard rules, and check the platform's
current help page if a user hits an upload error.

## Formats

| Platform | Best shape | Render as | Sweet-spot length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 9:16 | `9x16` 1080x1920 | 15–35s | Autoplays with sound, but many watch muted, so burn in captions |
| Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts | 9:16 | `9x16` | 15–45s | Same file as TikTok works |
| WhatsApp Status | 9:16 | `9x16` | ≤30s per status segment | Long videos get split, so keep it short. Keep file small (≤15 MB) for forwarding |
| LinkedIn feed | 4:5 or 1:1 on mobile, 16:9 on desktop | `4x5` (mobile-first) or `16x9` | 30–60s | Autoplays muted, so captions are essential. B2B buyers: lead with the business problem |
| LinkedIn / YouTube / presentations | 16:9 | `16x9` 1920x1080 | 30–90s | Use for website, email, and meetings |

Default pair for "LinkedIn and TikTok": `9x16` (TikTok) + `4x5` (LinkedIn mobile feed). Add `16x9` if the user also wants
it for YouTube, the website, or presentations.

## Safe zones (9:16)

TikTok covers the top ~11% (tabs), the bottom ~24% (caption, music, profile), and the right ~14% (like/comment/share
buttons). The renderer already keeps captions and screenshots out of these areas. Don't place logos, prices or
CTAs there in custom images either.

## Hook and pacing

- First 1–2 seconds decide whether people keep scrolling. Open with the pain or the promise, not a logo.
  Good: "Still re-typing invoices into Pastel?" Weak: "Introducing STEPEDGE IBMS".
- One idea per scene, 2.5–6 seconds each. Caption ≤ 7 words where possible: it has to be read in one glance on a phone.
- End with one clear CTA (book a demo / WhatsApp us / link in bio) held for 3–4 seconds.
- TikTok rewards a native feel: the faster pace, shorter total, and a question hook. LinkedIn rewards clarity and
  business outcomes (time saved, errors avoided, money visible).

## Post copy

LinkedIn post (under ~1,300 characters so the hook isn't cut by "…see more"):
1. Hook line (the problem), a blank line, then 3 short benefit bullets.
2. One-line proof or context ("Live at a 2-branch fashion retailer since October").
3. CTA, then 3–5 hashtags (#SmallBusiness #RetailTech #SouthAfrica …).

TikTok caption: one punchy line plus 3–5 hashtags. Put the CTA in the video itself; captions get truncated.

Also give the user: a suggested cover frame (the clearest scene), and the best posting time is something they should
test. Don't claim a specific "best time" as fact.

## Music

Use only music the user owns or has rights to. A video with copyrighted music can be muted or removed by
the platform. On TikTok, the in-app commercial music library is the safe route for business accounts:
render without music (or with a licensed track) and tell the user they can add sound in the app.
